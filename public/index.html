Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Stripe and x402 pricing?+
Stripe offers predictable monthly subscriptions with set limits, perfect for teams and businesses. x402 offers pay-as-you-go micropayments using crypto, ideal for AI agents, developers testing, and users who prefer granular control over spending.
Can I switch between plans?+
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your Stripe plan anytime. For x402, you can switch to Stripe monthly billing or vice versa. We'll pro-rate charges accordingly.
What chains are supported?+
Currently: Polkadot, Kusama, Westend, and Asset Hub. Coming soon: Ethereum, Base, Bitcoin, Solana, NEAR, Cosmos Hub, and SUI. All plans include multi-chain support.
